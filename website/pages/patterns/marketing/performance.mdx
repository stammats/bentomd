## Why This Works

- Six months of data shows a clear trend without overcrowding the chart
- `showValues: true` lets the audience read exact numbers
- The summary states the key insight ("improving efficiency") so the chart confirms rather than explains
- An upward trend is immediately visible, reinforcing the narrative
