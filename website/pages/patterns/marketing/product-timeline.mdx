## Why This Works

- The `active: true` flag highlights the current phase, giving the audience immediate orientation
- Each milestone has a date, title, and one-sentence description -- consistent structure
- 4 milestones is the sweet spot for a readable timeline
- Descriptions include specific deliverables, not vague promises
