## Why This Works

- Metrics follow the marketing funnel: impressions -> clicks -> conversions -> ROI
- Trend values compare against goals/benchmarks rather than just showing change
- The summary frames the campaign scope (6 weeks, multi-channel) and verdict (exceeded targets)
- Red/orange palette matches the energy of a performance review
