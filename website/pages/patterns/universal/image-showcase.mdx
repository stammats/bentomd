*Universal*

A full image slide with heading, summary, and caption. Use for product screenshots, architecture diagrams, and visual demonstrations.

```yaml
---
title: "Image Showcase"
aspectRatio: "16:9"
palette:
  primary: "#2563eb"

  footer: true
  pageNumber: true
```

## Why This Works

- The heading and summary provide context before the viewer sees the image
- `fit: contain` ensures the full image is visible without cropping
- The caption adds a specific detail that the image alone might not convey
- This pattern works for any visual: screenshots, diagrams, photos, mockups
