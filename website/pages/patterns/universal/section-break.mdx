# Section Break

*Universal*

A dark interstitial slide that separates major sections of a presentation. Minimal content -- just a title and tagline.


## Why This Works

- The dark background creates a strong visual break that signals a topic change
- Minimal content lets the audience mentally reset before the next section
- The subtitle previews what's coming, building anticipation
- No footer, header, or page number -- this slide is a pause, not content
