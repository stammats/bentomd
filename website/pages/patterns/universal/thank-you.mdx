## Why This Works

- Dark background matches the cover slide, creating a visual bookend for the deck
- "Questions? Let's talk." is more inviting than just "Q&A"
- Contact info is on two lines: personal details, then company details
- The `end` layout is similar to `cover` but semantically marks the deck's conclusion
