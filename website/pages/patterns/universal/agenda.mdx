## Source

```yaml
---
title: "Agenda"
aspectRatio: "16:9"
palette:
  primary: "#2563eb"

  pageNumber: true
---

---

pageNumber: true
---

2. **Product Roadmap** — Key releases shipped in Q1 and Q2 priorities (15 min)

3. **Customer Insights** — NPS trends, churn analysis, and top feature requests (10 min)

4. **Go-to-Market** — Pipeline health, win rates, and channel performance (10 min)

5. **Open Discussion** — Questions, risks, and cross-functional dependencies (10 min)
```

## Why This Works

- Each item follows the pattern: bold topic + dash + description + time allocation
- Time allocations show the meeting is planned and respect attendees' schedules
- 5 items is the right number for a 60-minute meeting
- The summary includes the meeting type and total duration for context
