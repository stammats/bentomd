## Options

| Option | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `background` | `string` | palette background | Background color or gradient |
| `align` | `string` | `"center"` | Text alignment |
| `color` | `string` | auto | Text color (auto-detects light/dark based on background) |

## Tips

- The `#` heading becomes the main title, `##` becomes the subtitle
- Any text below the subtitle becomes the attribution line (author, date, event)
- Use a dark `background` with light palette text for high-impact covers
- Keep titles to 3 lines max, subtitles to 2 lines
- The cover layout does not show headers, footers, or page numbers
